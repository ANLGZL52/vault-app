# Privacy Policy — Vault: Kendini Keşfet

**Effective Date:** June 25, 2026  
**Developer:** Anıl Güzel

## Overview

Vault: Kendini Keşfet ("the App") is a personality discovery app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## Data We Collect

### Account Data
- **Email address** — used for account creation and authentication via Firebase Auth.
- **Password** — stored securely (hashed) by Firebase; we never see or store your plain-text password.

### User Content
- **Personality test responses** — answers you provide to discover your personal insights, stored in your account on Firebase Firestore.
- **Vault activity** — which vaults you have opened and the insights generated for you, stored per account.

### Purchase History
- **In-app purchase records** — managed by RevenueCat on behalf of Apple; we store which packages you have purchased to unlock vault credits.

### Usage & Diagnostics
- **App crash reports and basic usage events** — collected anonymously via Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics to improve app stability.

## How We Use Your Data

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Email | Authentication, account recovery |
| Personality responses | Generate personalised AI-powered insights |
| Purchase history | Restore purchases across devices |
| Crash reports | Fix bugs and improve performance |

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the following service providers that operate on our behalf:

- **Google Firebase** — authentication, database, analytics (Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **RevenueCat** — in-app purchase management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Apple** — App Store payment processing

## Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at anlgzl52@gmail.com. Deletion is processed within 30 days.

## Children's Privacy

The App is rated 9+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest via Firebase) to protect your data.

## Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at anlgzl52@gmail.com.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
